Area of shapes

  1. Split the compound shape into rectangles and triangles.
  2. Find missing lengths by adding or subtracting.
  3. Area of each piece.
  4. Add them. Unit squared, like cm².

Rectangle: l × w.   Triangle: ½ × b × h.   Trapezium: ½ × (a + b) × h.   Parallelogram: b × h.

L-shape 10 by 8 with a 6 by 5 corner missing: 80 − 30 = 50 cm².

Mistakes to avoid

  • Multiplying before finding the missing length.
  • Using a sloping side as the height.
