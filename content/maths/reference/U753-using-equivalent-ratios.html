Using equivalent ratios

  1. Write the known ratio above the one you want, parts matching.
  2. Multiplier = new part ÷ known part.
  3. Multiply the other part by the same multiplier.
  4. Check both parts changed by the same number.

3:5 = 12:? → 12 ÷ 3 = 4 → 5 × 4 = 20.   300 g for 4 people, 10 people → ×2.5 → 750 g.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding the same number to both parts instead of multiplying.
  • Taking the multiplier from the wrong part.
