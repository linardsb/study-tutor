Percentage of an amount

No calculator

  1. 10%: divide by 10.
  2. 5%: halve the 10%.
  3. 1%: divide by 100.
  4. Build the percentage from those blocks and add.

35% of 80: 10% = 8, 30% = 24, 5% = 4, total 28.

With a calculator

Percentage as a decimal, then multiply: 0.35 × 80 = 28.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing up "of" with "increase". Increase adds the percentage on (U554).
  • Halving the amount instead of halving the 10% to get 5%.

Goes with U554 percentage increase, U332 repeated percentage change, U176 fractions, decimals and percentages.
