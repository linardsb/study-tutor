Velocity-time graphs

  1. Time along, velocity up. Check the units.
  2. Speed at a time: up to the line, then across.
  3. Acceleration = gradient = change in velocity ÷ time taken (m/s²).
  4. Flat = constant speed. Sloping down = slowing.
  5. Distance = area under the graph. Triangles and rectangles, then add (m).

0 to 20 m/s in 10 s: acceleration 2 m/s². Then 20 s flat, then 10 s to stop: 100 + 400 + 100 = 600 m.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Reading a flat line as stopped.
  • Reading the height of the line as the distance.
