Volume of a sphere

The exam gives you the formula. You do the steps.

  1. Radius. Halve a diameter first.
  2. Cube it: r × r × r.
  3. Divide by 3, multiply by 4.
  4. Multiply by π. Leave π in unless told a number.
  5. Unit cubed, like cm³.

V = 4/3 πr³   hemisphere = half of that

Radius 3 cm: 27 ÷ 3 = 9, 9 × 4 = 36, volume 36π cm³ (113.1 cm³ to 1 d.p.).

Mistakes to avoid

  • Squaring the radius instead of cubing it.
  • Dropping the 4/3, or using the diameter as r.
