Volume of a cone

The exam gives you the formula. You do the steps.

  1. Radius of the base. Halve a diameter first.
  2. Square it: r × r.
  3. Multiply by the perpendicular height h.
  4. Divide by 3.
  5. Multiply by π. Leave π in unless told a number.
  6. Unit cubed, like cm³.

V = 1/3 πr²h

Radius 3 cm, height 10 cm: 9 × 10 = 90, 90 ÷ 3 = 30, volume 30π cm³ (94.2 cm³ to 1 d.p.).

Mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping the ÷ 3. That gives a cylinder.
  • Using the slant height. h is the straight-up height.
