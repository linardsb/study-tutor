Pressure, force and area

  1. Triangle: F on top, P and A underneath.
  2. Cover the letter you want.
  3. Side by side: multiply. One above the other: divide.
  4. Work it out. Write the unit.

P = F ÷ A   F = P × A   A = F ÷ P

600 N on 2 m²: 600 ÷ 2 = 300 N/m².   50 N/m² on 4 m²: 50 × 4 = 200 N.

Units

Force in N. Area in m² gives N/m²; area in cm² gives N/cm².

Mistakes to avoid

  • Putting area on top. Force goes on top.
  • Mixing cm² and m² in one sum.
