Surface area of a pyramid

Square base plus four matching triangles. Five faces.

  1. Base: side × side.
  2. One triangle: ½ × base × slant height.
  3. Times 4.
  4. Add the base and the four triangles.
  5. Unit squared, like cm².

Base 6 cm, slant 5 cm: 36 + 4 × 15 = 96 cm².

Sloping faces only, or open at the bottom: leave out the base.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting the base, or the ½ in each triangle.
  • Using the perpendicular height instead of the slant height.
