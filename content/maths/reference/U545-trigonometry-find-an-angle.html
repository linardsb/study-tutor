Trigonometry: find an angle

  1. Label H, O and A from the angle you want.
  2. Pick SOH, CAH or TOA from the two sides you have.
  3. Write the fraction, numbers in, and simplify.
  4. No calculator: find the value in the table on the U283 sheet, read off the angle.
  5. Calculator: shift, sin, cos or tan, the fraction, equals.
  6. Answer in degrees.

O = 5, H = 10: sin θ = ½, θ = 30°.   O = 4, A = 4: tan θ = 1, θ = 45°.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Fraction upside down: top letter over bottom.
  • Reading the sin row when you used cos.
