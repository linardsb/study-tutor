Identifying graphs

  1. Highest power of x decides the shape.
  2. x only: straight line. x²: U shape. x³: S shape. 1/x: two curves.
  3. A minus in front of x² or x³ flips the shape.
  4. y = mx + c: m is the gradient, c is the y-intercept.
  5. Positive m goes up, negative m goes down.

y = 3x − 2: a line, up, crossing the y-axis at −2.   y = 7 − 3x: gradient −3.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Dropping the minus sign on the gradient.
  • Calling a U shape a straight line.

Goes with U377 parallel lines, U669 straight line graphs.
