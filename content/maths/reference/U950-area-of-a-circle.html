Area of a circle

  1. Radius. Halve a diameter first.
  2. Square it: r × r.
  3. Multiply by π. Leave π in unless told a number.
  4. Unit squared, like cm².

A = πr²   d = 2r

Diameter 8 cm: r = 4, r² = 16, area 16π cm². With π as 3: 48 cm².

With a calculator

Press the π key, round as asked. 16π = 50.3 cm² to 1 d.p.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using the diameter as the radius.
  • Doubling r instead of squaring it. πd is the circumference, not the area.
