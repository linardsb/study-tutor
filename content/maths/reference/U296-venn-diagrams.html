Venn diagrams

  1. "Both" goes in the overlap first.
  2. Set total − both = the "only" part of that circle.
  3. Total − everything inside the circles = outside.
  4. "And" is the overlap (A ∩ B). "Or" is both circles (A ∪ B). A' is not A.
  5. Probability = region ÷ total.

20 asked, 12 football, 9 tennis, 5 both: only parts 7 and 4, outside 4. P(tennis only) = 4/20 = 1/5.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Writing the set total in the crescent instead of taking the overlap off first.
  • Leaving the outside blank.
