Density, mass and volume

  1. Triangle: M on top, D and V underneath.
  2. Cover the letter you want.
  3. Side by side: multiply. One above the other: divide.
  4. Cuboid sides given? Volume first.
  5. Work it out. Write the unit.

D = M ÷ V   M = D × V   V = M ÷ D

540 g, 200 cm³: 540 ÷ 200 = 2.7 g/cm³.   8 g/cm³, 50 cm³: 8 × 50 = 400 g.

Units

g and cm³ give g/cm³. kg and m³ give kg/m³.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Dividing volume by mass. Mass goes on top.
  • Skipping the volume step.
