Trigonometry: find a side

  1. Label from the angle: H opposite the right angle, O across from it, A next to it.
  2. Pick SOH, CAH or TOA from the side you have and the side you want.
  3. Numbers in. Unknown on top: multiply. On the bottom: divide.

sin = O ÷ H   cos = A ÷ H   tan = O ÷ A

30°, H = 10: O = 10 × ½ = 5 cm.   30°, O = 7: H = 7 ÷ ½ = 14 cm.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Labelling from the wrong angle.
  • Multiplying when the unknown is on the bottom.
