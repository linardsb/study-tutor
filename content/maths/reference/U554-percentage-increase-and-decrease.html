Percentage increase and decrease

No calculator

  1. Find the percentage of the amount (10%, 5%, 1% blocks).
  2. Increase: add it on. Decrease: take it off.
  3. Unit, then a sense check.

£240 up 15%: 24 + 12 = 36, so 240 + 36 = £276.   £60 down 30%: 18 off, so £42.

With a calculator

Multiplier. Increase 15%: × 1.15. Decrease 30%: × 0.7.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Stopping at the percentage instead of adding or taking it off.
  • Using 0.3 instead of 0.7 for a 30% decrease.
