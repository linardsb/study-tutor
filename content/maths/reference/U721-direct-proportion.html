Direct proportion

  1. Value of one: cost ÷ number.
  2. Multiply by the number you want.
  3. Units, then a sense check.
  4. Best buy: cost of the same amount (100 g or one item) for each. Lower wins.

5 pens £4: one is £0.80, so 12 are £9.60.   12 cakes need 180 g: 15 g each, so 20 cakes need 300 g.

400 g for £1.20 = 30p per 100 g. 1 kg for £2.80 = 28p per 100 g. The 1 kg bag wins.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding instead of scaling.
  • Comparing price tags instead of the same amount.
