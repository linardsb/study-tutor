Equations of parallel lines

  1. Write each line as y = mx + c. Rearrange if needed.
  2. m is the number in front of x, sign included.
  3. Same m: parallel. Different m: not.
  4. For a parallel line, keep m and change c.
  5. Through a point: put x and y in, solve for c.

Parallel to y = 2x + 1 through (3, 10): 10 = 2 × 3 + c → c = 4 → y = 2x + 4.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Reading m from 2y = 4x + 6 as 4. It is 2.
  • Matching c instead of m.
