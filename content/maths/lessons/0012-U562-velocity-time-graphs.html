Velocity-time graphs

By the end you can find acceleration from the slope of a velocity-time graph and distance from the area.

A velocity-time graph shows how fast something is going at each moment. Time along the bottom, velocity (speed in a given direction) up the side. A car speeds up, cruises, then slows to a stop.

Sloping up means speeding up. Flat means the same speed. Sloping down means slowing. The shaded area is the distance: A + B + C.

Worked example

The graph above. (a) Velocity at 5 s. (b) Acceleration in the first 10 s. (c) Total distance.
Step 1, axes: time in seconds, velocity in m/s.
Step 2, (a) go up from 5 s to the line, across to the axis: 10 m/s.
Step 3, (b) change in velocity ÷ time: (20 − 0) ÷ 10 = 2, so 2 m/s².
Step 5, (c) split the area: triangle A, rectangle B, triangle C.
Step 6, A: ½ × 10 × 20 = 100. B: 20 × 20 = 400. C: ½ × 10 × 20 = 100.
Add: 100 + 400 + 100 = 600, so 600 m.

The method

  1. Read the axes and their units: time in seconds, velocity in metres per second (m/s).
  2. Velocity at a time: go up from that time to the line, then across to the velocity axis.
  3. Acceleration (how quickly velocity changes) is the gradient: change in velocity ÷ time taken. Unit m/s².
  4. A flat line has gradient 0, so constant speed. A line sloping down means slowing down.
  5. Distance travelled = area under the graph. Split it into triangles and rectangles.
  6. Triangle: ½ × base × height. Rectangle: base × height. Add them. Answer in metres.
Why is the area under the line the distance?

Distance is speed × time. On the graph, speed is the height and time is the width, and height × width is area. A triangle averages the changing speed for you.

Another way to see it

The whole shaded shape is a trapezium (four sides, one pair parallel). Area is ½ × (a + b) × h with a and b the parallel sides: ½ × (40 + 20) × 20 = 600 m. One sum instead of three.

The trapezium stops matching when the top is not flat, or the graph has two different top speeds. Then go back to splitting.

Watch out

Reading a flat line as "stopped". Flat means the same speed. Stopped is a line along the time axis, at velocity 0.

Reading the height as the distance. The top value, 20, is the speed. The distance is the area under the line.

Try it

Answer from memory, no calculator. Give the number only: m/s for velocity, m/s² for acceleration, metres for distance. The working appears only after you check.

Teach it back

A graph rises from 0 to 12 m/s in 6 seconds, then stays at 12 m/s for 10 seconds. Explain to a friend how to find the acceleration and the total distance. Say it out loud, or write it down.
