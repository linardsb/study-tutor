Ratios, fractions and percentages

By the end you can switch between fractions, decimals and percentages, and put a mixed list in order.

Fractions, decimals and percentages (per cent means out of 100) are three ways to write one number. 3/4, 0.75 and 75% are the same amount.

Each side is one move. Fraction to decimal: divide. Decimal to percentage: × 100. Percentage to fraction: over 100, simplify.

Worked example

Write 3/8 as a decimal and as a percentage.
Step 1, fraction to decimal: 3 ÷ 8 by short division. 8 into 3.000 gives 0.375.
Step 2, decimal to percentage: 0.375 × 100 = 37.5. So 37.5%.

Write 0.6, 2/3, 65% and 3/4 in order, smallest first.
Step 8, change them all to decimals.
Step 1 for 2/3: 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666... and for 3/4: 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
Step 3 for 65%: 65 ÷ 100 = 0.65.
Step 8 again: order the decimals 0.6, 0.65, 0.666..., 0.75.
Write the originals in that order: 0.6, 65%, 2/3, 3/4.

The method

  1. Fraction to decimal: divide the top by the bottom, or make the bottom 10, 100 or 1000.
  2. Decimal to percentage: multiply by 100. Every digit moves two places left.
  3. Percentage to decimal: divide by 100. Every digit moves two places right.
  4. Percentage to fraction: write it over 100, then simplify (divide top and bottom by the same number).
  5. Decimal to fraction: read the place value, then simplify. 0.35 = 35/100 = 7/20.
  6. Fraction to percentage: make the bottom 100, or go through the decimal.
  7. Ratio to fraction: add the parts for the bottom. In 3:2 the first part is 3/5.
  8. Order a mixed list: change all to decimals, order those, then write the originals in that order.

Learn these by heart. 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%. 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%. 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%. 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%. 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%. 1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5%. 1/3 = 0.333... (recurring, the 3s never stop) = 33.3%.

Why does multiplying by 100 turn a decimal into a percentage?

Per cent means out of 100. 0.75 is 75 hundredths, which is 75 out of 100. Multiplying by 100 counts the hundredths.

Another way to see it

Draw a hundred square: 10 rows of 10. Shade 75 squares. That is 75 out of 100: 75%, 0.75, three quarters.

75 green squares out of 100.

The square stops matching for 1/3. You cannot shade 33.3 squares, which is why 1/3 is a recurring decimal.

Watch out

Copying the fraction's digits as the decimal. 3/8 is 0.375, not 0.38 or 3.8. You have to divide.

Moving one place instead of two. 0.5 is 50%, not 5%.

Try it

Answer from memory, no calculator. Each question says which form to give. Write fractions like 9/20. The working appears only after you check.

1. Write 7/20 as a percentage. The first steps are done.

Step 6: make the bottom 100. 20 × 5 = 100, so multiply the top by 5 as well: 7 × 5 = …

Work out 7 × 5. That many out of 100 is the percentage.

Step 6: make the bottom 100. 20 × 5 = 100, so multiply the top by 5 as well: 7 × 5 = 35. 7/20 = 35/100 = 35%.

2. Write 0.45 as a fraction in its simplest form. The first step is done.

Step 5: read the place value. 0.45 is 45 hundredths, so 45/100. …

Which number divides into both 45 and 100?

Step 5: read the place value. 0.45 is 45 hundredths, so 45/100. Simplify: 5 divides into both. 45 ÷ 5 = 9 and 100 ÷ 5 = 20, so 9/20.

3. Write 5/8 as a percentage.

Divide 5 by 8 with short division first.

5 ÷ 8: 8 into 5.000 gives 0.625. 0.625 × 100 = 62.5. So 62.5%.

4. Find 35% of £60.

10% of 60 is 6. Build 35% from that.

This is percentage of an amount. 10% of 60 = 6. 30% = 18. 5% = 3. 35% = 18 + 3 = 21. So £21.

5. Three shops sell the same trainers at the same full price. Shop A takes 1/4 off, shop B takes 0.3 off and shop C takes 27% off. Which shop takes the most off? Answer A, B or C.

Turn all three into percentages first.

All as percentages: 1/4 = 25%, 0.3 = 30%, 27%. The biggest is 30%, so shop (b).
