Surface area of a pyramid

By the end you can find a square-based pyramid's surface area from its base and slant height.

Surface area is the total area of all the faces (the flat surfaces). A square-based pyramid has five: one square and four matching triangles. The net (the shape unfolded flat) shows all five. The slant height is the height of one triangle, from the middle of the base edge to the point.

Base 6 cm, slant height 5 cm. Square 36. Each triangle ½ × 6 × 5 = 15. Total 36 + 60 = 96 cm².

Unfold the net

Drag to turn it. Slide Unfold to lay the four triangles flat: that is the net. The blue line on the front face is the slant height. It stays the same length folded or flat.

Grey dashed line, only when folded: the perpendicular height. It is shorter than the slant height and it belongs in volume questions, not here.

Worked example

A square-based pyramid has base 6 cm and slant height 5 cm. Find its surface area.

Step 1, the base: 6 × 6 = 36.
Step 2, one triangle: ½ × 6 × 5 = 15.
Step 3, four triangles: 4 × 15 = 60.
Step 4, add: 36 + 60 = 96.
Step 5, the unit: 96 cm².

The method

  1. Area of the square base: side × side.
  2. Area of one triangle: ½ × base × slant height.
  3. Multiply that by 4.
  4. Add the base and the four triangles.
  5. Add the unit squared, like cm².

If a question wants the sloping faces only, or the pyramid is open, leave out the base.

Why use the slant height for the triangle, and not the height of the pyramid?

Each triangle is a flat face, so its height is measured on that face: the slant height. The pyramid's own height runs up the inside, where there is no face, and is shorter.

Watch out

Count the base. Four triangles alone is not the whole surface, unless the question says so.

Keep the ½. 6 × 5 = 30 is two triangles, not one.

The slant height belongs to the face. The pyramid's own height is shorter.

Try it
