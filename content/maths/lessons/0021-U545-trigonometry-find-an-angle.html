Trigonometry: find an angle

By the end you can find a missing angle in a right-angled triangle from two sides.

Two sides given and the angle missing: the same labels work. H is opposite the right angle, O is across from the angle you want, A is next to it. Write the ratio as a fraction and simplify it. On Paper 1 the fraction is in the exact-value table. On a calculator the inverse buttons sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ turn a ratio back into an angle.

O = 5, H = 10. sin θ = 5 ÷ 10 = ½. sin 30° = ½, so θ = 30°.

Which ratio, and then undo it

The start is the same as finding a side. The difference is the last step: you have the fraction, so you undo the ratio with the inverse button to get the angle.

Same shape as the U283 diagram, with the ending changed.

Worked example

The side opposite angle θ is 5 cm. The hypotenuse is 10 cm. Find θ.

Step 1, label from the angle: O = 5, H = 10.
Step 2, the ratio with O and H: SOH.
Step 3, the fraction: sin θ = 5 ÷ 10.
Step 4, simplify: 5 ÷ 10 = ½.
Step 5, the table: sin 30° = ½.
Step 6, with a calculator instead: shift, sin, 5 ÷ 10, equals. The screen shows 30.
Step 7, the answer: θ = 30°.

The method

  1. Label H, O and A from the angle you want.
  2. Pick the ratio from the two sides you have: SOH, CAH or TOA.
  3. Write the fraction: top letter over bottom letter, numbers in.
  4. Simplify the fraction.
  5. No calculator: find that value in the table and read off the angle.
  6. Calculator: shift, then sin, cos or tan, type the fraction, equals.
  7. Write the angle in degrees.

Why do you need to know which ratio you used before reading the table?

½ is in the table twice: sin 30° and cos 60°. The fraction alone does not name the angle. The ratio from step 2 picks the row, and the row gives the angle.

Watch out

Keep the fraction the right way up. sin is O over H: 5 ÷ 10, not 10 ÷ 5.

Read the right row. ½ is sin 30° but also cos 60°. Match the ratio you used.

Answer in degrees. ½ is the ratio, not the angle.

Not an angle: this is find a side. Have H, want A, so CAH. cos 60° = x ÷ 8, and cos 60° = ½. x = 8 × ½ = 4 cm.

5. Sam finds the angle in this triangle. Step 1: A = 4, H = 8. Step 2: SOH. Step 3: sin θ = 4 ÷ 8. Step 4: ½. Step 5: sin 30° = ½, so θ = 30°. Which step went wrong first? Answer with the step number.

Which two sides does Sam have? Check each step against them.

The sides are A and H, so the ratio is CAH, not SOH. cos θ = 4 ÷ 8 = ½, and cos 60° = ½, so the angle is 60°. First wrong step = 2.
