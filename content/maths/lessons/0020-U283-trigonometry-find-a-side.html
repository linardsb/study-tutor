Trigonometry: find a side

By the end you can label a right-angled triangle, pick the ratio and find a missing side.

Trigonometry (trig) links an angle in a right-angled triangle to its sides. Label the sides from the angle given. The hypotenuse (H) is the longest side, opposite the right angle. The opposite (O) is across from the angle. The adjacent (A) is next to it.

Angle 30°, H = 10 cm. sin 30° = O ÷ H, so O = 10 × ½ = 5 cm.

Which ratio?

The hard step is choosing SOH, CAH or TOA. Follow the arrows: the two letters you ticked decide it, and where the unknown sits decides multiply or divide.

Worked example

Angle 30°, hypotenuse 10 cm. Find the side opposite the angle.

Step 1, label from the 30°: H = 10, O is wanted, A is the bottom.
Step 2, tick: have H, want O.
Step 3, the ratio with O and H: SOH.
Step 4, write it: sin 30° = O ÷ 10, and sin 30° = ½.
Step 5, O is on top, so multiply: O = 10 × ½ = 5.
Step 6, the unit: 5 cm.

Angle 30°, the side opposite it is 7 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

Step 2, have O, want H. Step 3, SOH.
Step 4, ½ = 7 ÷ H.
Step 5, H is on the bottom, so divide: H = 7 ÷ ½ = 14.
Step 6: 14 cm.

The method

  1. Label H, O and A from the angle you know.
  2. Tick the side you have and the side you want.
  3. Pick the ratio with those two letters: SOH, CAH or TOA.
  4. Write it out with the numbers in.
  5. Unknown on top: multiply. Unknown on the bottom: divide.
  6. Add the unit.

SOH CAH TOA: sin = O ÷ H, cos = A ÷ H, tan = O ÷ A. Paper 1 angles are 30°, 45° or 60°: use the table. Calculator: check degrees, type sin 30, round.

Why is sin 30° always a half, whatever the size of the triangle?

Every 30° right-angled triangle is the same shape, just bigger or smaller. Scaling multiplies all three sides by the same number, so O ÷ H stays ½.

Watch out

Label from the angle in the question. Swap the angle and O and A swap.

Unknown on the bottom: divide. sin 30° = 7 ÷ H gives H = 14, not 3.5.

sin 30° is ½, but cos 30° is √3 ÷ 2. Read row and column.

5. A ramp rises 3 m and makes an angle of 30° with the ground. How long is the ramp, in metres?

The ramp is H. sin 30° = 3 ÷ H.

The height is O, across from the 30°. The ramp is H. Step 3: SOH. Step 4: ½ = 3 ÷ H. Step 5: H is on the bottom, so divide: H = 3 ÷ ½ = 6. Step 6: 6 m.
