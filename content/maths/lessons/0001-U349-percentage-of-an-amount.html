Percentage of an amount

By the end you can find any percentage of an amount without a calculator, and check it with one.

Per cent means "out of 100". So 35% is 35 parts out of 100 equal parts. Cutting into 10 blocks is quicker than 100. Each block is 10%.

8888888888

80 split into ten equal blocks. One block is 10%, which is 8. The shaded part is 35%: three blocks and a half.

Worked example

Find 35% of 80.

Step 1, find 10%: 80 ÷ 10 = 8.
Step 2, find 5%: half of 8 = 4.
Step 4, build 35% from the blocks: 10% + 10% + 10% + 5% = 8 + 8 + 8 + 4 = 28.

The method

  1. Find 10%: divide the amount by 10.
  2. Find 5%: halve your 10%.
  3. Find 1%: divide the amount by 100.
  4. Build the percentage you need from those blocks, then add them up.

With a calculator, Papers 2 and 3: turn the percentage into a decimal and multiply. 0.35 × 80 = 28.

Why is 5% half of your 10%, and not half of the amount?

5% is half of 10%, so you halve the 10% answer. Half of 80 is 40, and 40 is 50% of 80, not 5%.

Another way to see it

A percentage is also a multiplier. 35% is 0.35, so 35% of 80 is 0.35 × 80.

Two scales on one line. 35% of the way along the bottom is 28 on the top.

The multiplier stops matching the blocks there. It gives no 10% step, so on Paper 1 you still write out 0.35 × 80.

Watch out

"Percentage of" is not "percentage increase". Increase means find the percentage, then add it on. That is U554.

5% is half of your 10%, not half of the amount.
