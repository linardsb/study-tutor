Simplifying ratio

By the end you can simplify any ratio, even with mixed units or three parts. You can also write one as 1:n.

A ratio compares amounts. 12:18 means 12 of one thing for every 18 of another. Simplifying says the same comparison with the smallest whole numbers.

12:18 in equal groups of six. Two groups to three groups, so 12:18 is 2:3.

Worked example

Simplify 2 m : 50 cm.

Step 1, same units: 2 m is 200 cm, so 200 : 50.
Step 2, highest common factor of 200 and 50 is 50.
Step 3, divide every part: 200 ÷ 50 = 4 and 50 ÷ 50 = 1, giving 4:1.
Step 4, check: nothing except 1 divides into both 4 and 1, so 4:1 is done.

Three parts: simplify 8:12:20. Step 2, the highest common factor of all three is 4. Step 3, 2:3:5.

The method

  1. Make the units the same, then drop the unit.
  2. Find the highest common factor, HCF (the biggest number that divides into every part).
  3. Divide every part by it.
  4. Check: can anything else divide into all the parts? If not, it is simplified.

For the form 1:n, divide both parts by the first number. Decimals are allowed here, so 4:10 becomes 1:2.5.

Why divide every part by the same number, and not just the big one?

A ratio is a comparison, so both sides have to shrink together. 12:18 is two groups against three. Divide only the 18 and you have changed what is being compared, not how it is written.

Another way to see it

Write the ratio as a fraction and cancel it. 12:18 becomes 12/18, which cancels by 6 to 2/3.

Five equal blocks: two blue to three amber.

The fraction stops matching the bar at the whole. 2/3 compares blue with amber, but the bar has five blocks, so blue is 2/5 of everything.

Watch out

Units first. 2:50 is wrong for 2 m : 50 cm, because the two numbers are counting different things.

Divide every part by the same number. Dividing one part only changes the ratio.
