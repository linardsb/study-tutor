The four bars on a card are the ladder: learning, 1 pass, 2 passes, secure. A bar fills when a cold re-test is passed. Green here means the school saw it right once. A topic is counted as secure after passing cold re-tests in this folder at 3, 10 and 30 days, and it still gets re-tested every 60 days. Nothing on this page is a prediction.
